Which email should we send from?

Connect the mailbox you want your outreach to come from.

Using Gmail? You need an App Password (not your normal password): go to myaccount.google.com → Security → 2-Step Verification → App passwords, create one, and paste the 16-character code below.

Send your campaign

Double-check everything, then start. You can stop at any time.

Heads up: Gmail allows roughly 500 emails a day (2,000 on Workspace). Sending too fast can get your account flagged — 10 seconds between emails is a safe pace.

When a recipient replies, the AI picks up the conversation and negotiates the deal for you — either assisted (you approve each reply) or full autopilot (reads your inbox over IMAP and replies on its own). A deal thread opens for each person, on the Deals tab.

The AI opens near your minimum, never goes above your maximum, and never reveals the ceiling. It won't promise performance results.
